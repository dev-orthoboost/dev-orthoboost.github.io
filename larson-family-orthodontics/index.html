Good questions

Find an answer fast.

The things families ask us most, before their very first visit.

When should my child first see an orthodontist?
Around age 7, even if treatment is still years away. An early check lets us watch how the jaw and adult teeth are coming in and catch small things while they're simple to guide. It's free, and most kids won't need anything yet.
How much do braces or Invisalign cost?
It depends on the treatment, but you'll always see the full cost before anything begins. Most families combine insurance with a monthly plan, and we walk you through every number so there are no surprises.
Do you take my insurance?
We accept most major plans and file the paperwork for you. Bring your card to the first visit and we'll tell you exactly what's covered before you decide anything.
Will treatment hurt?
Today's braces and aligners are far gentler than they used to be. There can be mild soreness for a day or two after an adjustment, and we do everything we can to keep every visit calm and comfortable.
How long does treatment usually take?
Most cases run 12 to 24 months, though every smile is different. We'll give you an honest timeline up front and keep check-ins on a schedule that fits your family's week.
Do you treat adults too?
Absolutely. A good share of our patients are grown-ups, and clear aligners make it easy to straighten discreetly while you work and live. It's never too late.
