Common questions

How long do braces take?+

Most cases run 12 to 18 months. Your projected timeline comes from the 3D plan and is reviewed with you before treatment begins.

Do braces hurt?+

Most patients feel pressure for a few days after placement and after adjustments, then stop noticing them. Over-the-counter pain relief is usually enough.

Metal or ceramic?+

Ceramic brackets blend with the tooth and are far less visible; metal is slightly more efficient for some movements. We will show you both options against your case.
