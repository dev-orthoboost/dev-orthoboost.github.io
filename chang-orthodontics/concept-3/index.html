Answers, before you ask.

What ages do you treat?+

Everyone. Mostly kids and teens, and a growing number of adults. Treatment paths are tuned to each stage of growth.

When should my child first see an orthodontist?+

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first evaluation around age 7. Most children who come in at that age need nothing yet, but it tells us whether to treat, monitor, or do nothing.

Do I need a referral from my dentist?+

No. Many patients come to us through their dentist, but you can book a consultation directly.

What happens at the first visit?+

A free exam and digital scan, about 45 minutes. You leave understanding your options, your timeline, and your estimate, with no obligation.

How much does treatment cost?+

It depends on the plan. Your free consultation ends with a clear, itemized estimate in writing before any decision.

Do you take insurance?+

We work with most major plans, verify your orthodontic benefit, and file on your behalf so the coordination is not your job.
