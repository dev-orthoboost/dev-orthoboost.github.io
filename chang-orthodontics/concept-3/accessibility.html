Accessibility

Our commitment to a site that works for everyone.

This design is built to WCAG 2.1 AA intent: color contrast on text and interface elements, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks and headings, alt text on images, and full keyboard operation of menus and accordions.

Motion is limited by design, and the site honors the reduced-motion setting in your operating system.

If you encounter a barrier on the live site, call the practice at (650) 598-0888 and we will address it and help you get what you need in the meantime.
