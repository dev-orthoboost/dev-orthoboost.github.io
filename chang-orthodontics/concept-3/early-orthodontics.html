Early treatment, timed to growth.

Guiding jaw development while a child is still growing creates space for adult teeth and often prevents more complex treatment later.

How it works

Evaluate around age 7

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first check at seven, when the jaw is still developing and problems are cheapest to fix.

Create room

Expanders and space maintainers widen the arch or hold space so adult teeth have somewhere to go.

Reassess before phase 2

Many children need nothing further for years. We monitor rather than treat when monitoring is the right answer.

Common questions

What age is best for an expander?+

Expanders work best between roughly ages 7 and 14, while the upper jaw is still developing. An early evaluation tells us if and when to start.

Does my child definitely need phase 1 treatment?+

Often not. Plenty of children are evaluated, monitored, and never need early treatment. We will say so when that is the case.

Does an expander hurt?+

Most children feel pressure rather than pain for the first few days, and adapt to speaking and eating with it within about a week.
