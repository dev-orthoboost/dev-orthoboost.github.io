Common questions

How long do I have to wear a retainer?+

Full-time for several months after treatment, then nights indefinitely. Retention is not a phase that ends; it is what keeps the result.

I lost my retainer. What now?+

Call us. If we have your scan on file a replacement is quick. Do not wait, because teeth move fastest in the first weeks without retention.

How do I clean it?+

Rinse every time it comes out, brush gently with a soft brush, and keep it away from heat. Hot water warps clear retainers.
