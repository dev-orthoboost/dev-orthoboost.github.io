Diagnostics in three dimensions.

Every plan here starts as data: a digital scan, low-dose 3D imaging, and a simulation you can look at before treatment begins.

Digital scanning

A wand, not a tray of putty. The scan is comfortable, takes about ten minutes, and produces a precise 3D model of your bite.

Low-dose 3D imaging

Imaging shows roots, bone, and airway in one pass. It is how complex cases stop being guesswork.

Planning and monitoring

Dr. Chang simulates tooth movement digitally, then checks real progress against that plan at every visit.
