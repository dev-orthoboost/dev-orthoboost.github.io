Common questions

Are aligners as effective as braces?+

For many cases, yes. Complex bite corrections sometimes do better with fixed appliances, and we will tell you plainly if that is your case rather than sell you the tray.

How many hours a day do I wear them?+

Plan on 20 to 22 hours a day, out for meals and brushing. Aligners only work while they are in.

Is there an option for younger patients?+

Yes. Invisalign Teen and Invisalign First are designed for growing patients, including a palatal expander option.
