What happens when you come in

01

Welcome and paperwork

Short. We ask what brought you in and what you are hoping to change, then get you into the chair rather than the waiting room.

02

Exam and 3D scan

Dr. Chang examines the bite himself and takes a digital scan — no putty impressions. The scan shows roots and bone, not just the surface.

03

Your plan, in plain language

What is going on, what the options are, roughly how long each takes, and what each costs. Said in words, not chart notes.

What you will leave knowing

Nobody should have to decide about treatment in the chair. The point of the first visit is that you go home with enough to think it over properly.
