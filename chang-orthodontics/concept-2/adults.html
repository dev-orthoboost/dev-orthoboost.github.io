A growing share of this practice is adults, treating everything from crowding that got worse to bites that were never fully corrected the first time.

Discreet by default

Ceramic brackets and clear aligners mean most adult treatment is barely visible in meetings or photos.

Retreatment is common

Teeth that shifted after childhood braces are one of the most frequent adult cases, and limited treatment is often shorter than people fear.

Function, not just looks

Bite problems wear teeth down over decades. Correcting alignment is often as much about protecting the teeth as straightening them.

Common questions

Am I too old for orthodontics?+

No. Teeth move at any age, given healthy gums and bone. What changes with age is the approach, not whether it works.

Will people notice?+

With clear aligners, rarely. With ceramic brackets, far less than you expect. We will show you both against your own scan.

I had braces as a kid and they moved back. Now what?+

Very common, and usually because retention stopped. Limited retreatment plus proper retention often fixes it in well under a year.
