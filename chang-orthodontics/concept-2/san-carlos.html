Finding us

We are the northernmost building in San Carlos, right on the Belmont border, between the Holiday Inn Express and Hyatt Place on El Camino Real. Parking is on site.

Most of our families come from San Carlos and Belmont, and a good number drive down from Redwood City or up from San Mateo. After-school appointments exist because that drive has to fit around a school day.

The doctor you will actually see

Dr. Michael Chang is board-certified by the American Board of Orthodontics, holds a faculty appointment at UCSF, and has master's-level training in airway orthodontics. Every scan at this office is reviewed by him.

One office means one orthodontist. You are not handed between locations, and the person who plans your treatment is the person who finishes it.
