Answers, before you ask.

What ages do you treat?+

Everyone. Mostly kids and teens, and a growing number of adults. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first evaluation around age 7, and we time treatment to growth from there.

How much does treatment cost?+

It depends on the plan. Your free consultation includes a digital scan and a clear, itemized estimate. In-house payment plans and insurance coordination are available.

How long does treatment take?+

Most plans run 12 to 18 months. You will see the projected timeline in your 3D simulation before anything starts, and progress is checked against that plan at every visit.

Do you take insurance?+

We work with most major plans and handle the coordination for you, so your orthodontic benefit gets used fully.

What happens at the first visit?+

A free consultation and digital scan, about 45 minutes. You leave understanding your options, your timeline, and your estimate. No obligation.

Invisalign or braces?+

Both get excellent results in the right case. After your scan we will show you a simulation of each option and recommend the better fit for your goals.
