Something broke. Here is what to do.

Most orthodontic problems are uncomfortable rather than urgent, and nearly all of them wait safely until we can see you. Call (650) 598-0888 and we will tell you which one you have.

Common problems

We hold same-day appointments for existing patients with an active problem.

Loose or broken bracket

If it is still on the wire, leave it and cover it with orthodontic wax. If it comes off entirely, keep it and bring it in. Call us to see whether it needs an earlier visit.

Poking wire

Cover the end with wax. If it is long, a clean nail clipper can trim it behind the last bracket. Call us either way so we can smooth it properly.

Lost or cracked aligner

Go back to your previous tray and keep wearing it so teeth do not drift, then call. Do not skip ahead to the next tray without talking to us.

General soreness

Normal for a few days after placement or an adjustment. Wax on the sore spot, soft foods, and over-the-counter pain relief usually cover it.

Lost retainer

Call promptly. Teeth move fastest in the first weeks without retention, and a replacement is quick if we have your scan on file.

Injury to the mouth or jaw

A blow to the face, a tooth knocked loose, or an injury with bleeding is a medical matter first. Seek urgent medical or dental care, then call us about the appliance.

When in doubt, call.

You will not be wasting anyone's time. A 60-second phone call usually sorts out whether something needs an appointment today or can wait for your next visit.
