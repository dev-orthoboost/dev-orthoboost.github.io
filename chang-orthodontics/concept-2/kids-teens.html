Common questions

Is age 7 not too early?+

For treatment, usually yes. For an evaluation, no. At seven the jaw is still developing, and seeing it early is what lets us avoid bigger interventions later.

Will my teen be able to play sports?+

Yes. A mouthguard over braces is standard, and aligners come out for contact sports. Tell us what they play and we will fit the plan to it.

What if they do not wear their aligners?+

It shows up quickly at check-ins, and we would rather switch to fixed braces than let a plan quietly fail. That conversation is normal, not a failure.
