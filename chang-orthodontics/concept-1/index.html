Answers, Before You Ask

What ages do you treat?add

Everyone. We treat children, teens, and adults, with treatment paths tuned to each stage of growth. Early evaluations help us time treatment for the best, most efficient result.

How much does treatment cost?add

It depends on the plan. Your complimentary consult includes a 3D scan and a clear, itemized estimate, with no obligation. Flexible in-house financing is available to fit most budgets.

How long does treatment take?add

Most cases run 12 to 18 months. We map your timeline up front using a 3D simulation, so you know the plan before we begin, and digital monitoring keeps treatment on target at every visit.

Do you take insurance?add

We work with most major insurance plans and will help you maximize your orthodontic benefit. For any remaining balance, in-house, interest-friendly payment plans are available.

What happens at the first visit?add

A complimentary consultation and 3D scan: comfortable, quick, and putty-free. You will leave understanding your options, your timeline, and your estimate, with no pressure to commit.

Clear aligners or braces, which is right for me?add

Both can deliver excellent results, and the right choice depends on your case. After your 3D scan we will show you a predictive simulation for each option and recommend the best fit for your goals and lifestyle.
