How It Works

Assess the structure

Coordinate care

When findings fall outside orthodontics, we refer and work alongside your physician or dentist rather than treating past our lane.

Common Questions

What is airway-focused orthodontics?add

It considers how jaw and palate development relate to the nasal airway, alongside the usual goals of alignment and bite. It is an evaluation lens, not a separate product.

Is this a treatment for sleep apnea?add

No. Sleep apnea is diagnosed and managed by physicians. Orthodontic treatment can sometimes support that care, and we coordinate with your medical team rather than replacing it.

Can adults be treated this way?add

Sometimes. Adult palates are fused, so options differ from children and often involve skeletal approaches such as MARPE. The evaluation tells us what is realistic.
